## Canonical positioning

**Tourism Intelligence Desk is a public evidence-governed decision-support prototype that integrates two research cases with different epistemic states: reproduced HATI-Madrid research and real Sentinel-2 / derived SNTO evidence.**

Its differentiator is not generative fluency. It is the explicit separation of:

- observed evidence;
- derived indicators;
- model-derived outputs;
- reproduced results;
- validated evidence;
- operational authorization.

Unsupported claims return **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE** rather than fabricated findings.

## What the project demonstrates professionally

The project is evidence of applied work across:

- tourism intelligence and destination decision support;
- geospatial / Earth-observation research;
- evidence-state modelling and claim governance;
- reproducible research integration;
- deterministic analytical guardrails;
- React / TypeScript interface implementation;
- Git / GitHub workflow and CI regression tests;
- immutable provenance and public deployment;

## What not to claim

Do not describe Tourism Intelligence Desk as:

- an operational destination-management platform;
- a live sensor or satellite ingestion system;
- an autonomous AI decision-maker;
- a validated tourism-impact attribution engine;
- a system that predicts tourist behaviour;
- a tool authorized to issue closures, quotas or regulatory decisions.
